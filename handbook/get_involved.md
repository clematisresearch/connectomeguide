# Get Involved

**Connectome 2026–2027** is built on open science, community collaboration, and the belief that authentic research should be accessible to curious minds everywhere. Whether you are a student, educator, student club leader, academic association, researcher, or prospective sponsor, there are many meaningful ways to be part of this global initiative.

---

## 1. For Participants: Student Ambassador Program

Are you excited about connectomics and want to share your interest in neuroscience with others? Any student worldwide is eligible to become an official **Connectome Student Ambassador**!

To get started, engage with the competition and the wider community through **at least one** of the following routes:

### How to Qualify

1. **Route 1: Social Media & Public Outreach**
   Share at least **five posts or stories** about the competition across your social media channels (e.g., LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter, WeChat, YouTube, or school platforms), or encourage your school, teachers, or science clubs to share competition announcements.

2. **Route 2: Peer Mentorship & Inspiration**
   Encourage at least **2–3 classmates or peers** to participate in the competition or explore the connectomics tutorials and resources.

3. **Route 3: Self-Led Community Events**
   Organize an activity related to the competition, such as:

   * a high school or university journal club discussing the *Drosophila* or human connectome;
   * an informal study group or workshop helping peers explore Neuroglancer, neuPrint, Nilearn, or other competition tools; or
   * an outreach activity with a student club or organization to introduce other students to the competition.

### Becoming an Official Student Ambassador

Once you have completed **at least one** of the routes above, you can apply to become an official **Connectome Student Ambassador**.

Email us at `clematis.reh@gmail.com` with a brief description of what you have done and, where appropriate, links, screenshots, or other evidence of your activities.

Once approved, you will officially serve as a **Connectome Student Ambassador for the remainder of the competition period**.

As an ambassador, you will:

* receive an official **Certificate of Student Ambassador Recognition**;
* gain leadership experience in science communication, community organizing, and academic outreach; and
* be recognized on the **Clematis Discord and official competition channels**.

:::{important}

### Community Engagement Award

To be considered for the **Community Engagement Award**, you must qualify as an official **Connectome Student Ambassador at some point during the competition period**.

Continued and meaningful engagement with the community throughout the competition is highly encouraged!
:::

## 2. For Teachers, Educators, & Faculty

Educators play a transformative role in sparking scientific curiosity. By introducing connectomics and cutting-edge research to your classroom, you help students discover the excitement of real scientific inquiry.

### How You Can Support Your Students
* **Share the Competition:** Share our [website](https://clematisresearch.github.io/connectome/) and [Competition Handbook](index.md) and tutorials with your biology, computer science, psychology, or data science classes.
* **Encourage Authentic Inquiry:** Highlight how open-access datasets allow students to ask original research questions without requiring laboratory wet-lab infrastructure.
* **Integrate as Coursework / Extra Credit:** Use our open-access tutorials as supplementary learning modules or independent study projects.

### Faculty Ambassador Recognition
We are eager to celebrate educators who champion science education! Faculty members who encourage their students to participate will be recognized as official **Faculty Ambassadors** and have their name highlighted on our community platforms.

## 3. For Student Clubs & Societies

Are you part of a high school science club, pre-med society, undergraduate neuroscience association, or science research club? Partner with us to bring exciting workshops and research opportunities to your members!

We welcome student clubs and societies worldwide to partner with us and introduce their members to connectomics and the competition.

### Ways to Get Involved

We encourage clubs to explore a partnership that works best for your community. For example, you might:

* Host a Connectomics Workshop: Use our step-by-step guides and resources to introduce members to connectome datasets and tools.
* Form a Club Cohort: Bring interested members together to learn, discuss ideas, and work on their own individual competition projects.
* Share the Competition & Resources: Help spread the word through your club's social media, mailing lists, meetings, or school community.
* Create Your Own Activity: Organize a journal club, tutorial session, guest talk, or another connectomics-related activity that fits your members' interests.

Partner clubs will have their club name or logo featured on our website, Discord, or relevant event materials.

If your club would like to get involved, contact us at clematis.reh@gmail.com or reach out through the Clematis Discord. We are also happy to discuss other ideas for collaboration that suit your club and community.

## 4. For Associations & Outreach Partners

We collaborate with academic societies, non-profits, scientific networks, and educational organizations to expand access to neuroscience education.

### How Associations Can Partner
* **Network Outreach:** Share competition milestones, webinars, and opportunities through your newsletters, mailing lists, and social channels.
* **Event Collaboration:** Host joint webinars or panel discussions on open neuroscience, connectomics, and scientific career pathways.
* **Global Access:** Help us connect with students from underrepresented and geographically diverse backgrounds who can benefit from free research opportunities.

## 5. For Researchers, Pedagogical Experts, & Advisors

We warmly invite neuroscience researchers, connectomics specialists, pedagogical innovators, and science communicators to join us as **Faculty/Institutional Partners or Advisory Members**.

### As an Advisor or Partner, You Can:
* **Guide Pedagogical Excellence:** Help us refine our educational frameworks and ensure that our materials foster deep conceptual learning and scientific rigor.
* **Broaden Reach & Communication:** Share insights on making complex concepts accessible to everyone and reaching learners worldwide.
* **Curricular Integration:** Help identify pathways to integrate these open-access connectomics resources into secondary and higher-education curricula.

If you or your institution are interested in collaborating, please reach out to us at `clematis.reh@gmail.com`.

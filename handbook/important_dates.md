## Important Dates

The following dates are important milestones for Connectome 2026–2027:

| Event | Date |
|---------|---------|
| **Competition Period** | August 2026 – December 2027 |
| **Early Feedback Opportunity** | October 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT−4) |
| **Final Submission Deadline** | December 1, 2027, 5:00 PM EST (GMT−5) |
| **Winners and Awardees Announced** | April 2028 |
| **Certificates and Prize Distribution** | May–June 2028 |

## Early Feedback Opportunity

Participants who submit their work by **October 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT−4)** will receive **detailed feedback on their submitted work from the organizers**. This is an optional opportunity intended to help participants identify areas for improvement and refine their projects before the final submission deadline. We aim to provide this feedback by **January 2027**.

:::{note}

### Feedback Throughout the Competition

The Early Feedback Opportunity is not the only chance to receive guidance!

Throughout the competition, there will be many opportunities to interact with the **Competition Co-Chairs, guest speakers, and the wider Clematis community** through workshops, talks, help sessions, and Discord.

These sessions may cover **common mistakes, research strategies, tips and tricks, and general feedback** that can help participants at all stages of their projects.

And, as always, if you have questions while working on your project, you are welcome to ask us through the **Clematis Discord**. We are always happy to talk about **neuroscience, connectomics, your research ideas, or anything you are curious about!**

:::

## Final Submission Deadline

All competition submissions must be received by **December 1, 2027, 5:00 PM EST (GMT−5)**.

All required submission components—including video submissions posted on the Clematis Discord and written reports submitted through the appropriate Google Form(s)—must be received by the deadline.

Late submissions will not be accepted. To avoid potential issues with internet connectivity, file uploads, platform outages, or other unforeseen circumstances, we strongly recommend submitting your materials at least 24 hours before the deadline.

## Winners and Awardees Announced

Competition winners and award recipients will be announced in **April 2028**.

## Certificates and Prize Distribution

Participation certificates, award certificates, and prizes will be distributed between **May and June 2028**.

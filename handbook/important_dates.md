# Important Dates

The following dates are important milestones for Connectome2026:

| Event | Date |
|---------|---------|
| **Competition Period** | August–December 2026 |
| **Early Feedback Opportunity** | September 30, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT-4) |
| **Final Submission Deadline** | December 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EST (GMT−5) |
| **Winners and Awardees Announced** | March 2027 |
| **Certificates and Prize Distribution** | May–June 2027 |

## Early Feedback Opportunity

Participants who submit their work by **September 30, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT-4)** will receive general feedback from the organizers. This is an optional opportunity intended to help participants refine their projects before the final submission deadline. We aim to provide feedback by mid-November 2026. 

## Final Submission Deadline

All competition submissions must be received by **December 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EST (GMT-5)**.

All required submission components—including video submissions posted on the Clematis Discord and written reports submitted through the appropriate Google Form(s)—must be received by the deadline.

Late submissions will not be accepted. To avoid potential issues with internet connectivity, file uploads, platform outages, or other unforeseen circumstances, we strongly recommend submitting your materials at least 24 hours before the deadline.

## Winners and Awardees Announced

Competition winners and award recipients will be announced in **March 2027**.

## Certificates and Prize Distribution

Participation certificates, award certificates, and prizes will be distributed between **May and June 2027**.

# 2025 Fly Connectome Research Competition

In 2025, we hosted the **first International Synapse-Level Connectomics Competition**, which focused on reconstructing and analysing neural circuits in the **fly optic lobe** using electron microscopy (EM) data.

Although the scientific focus differs from this year's competition, many of the educational materials—particularly those related to **Track 1**—remain highly relevant. If you are interested in learning more about connectomics, we encourage you to explore the resources from last year's competition:

**Competition website:**
[https://flycomp2025.my.canva.site/](https://flycomp2025.my.canva.site/)

## Beyond Research Competitions

One of the goals of the competition is to encourage participants to undertake research that can contribute to the scientific community.

Following the 2025 competition, **Renoy Antony Olivera** further developed his competition project into a peer-reviewed research article:

> Olivera, R. A. (2026). Cross-Platform Neurotransmitter & Alias Ambiguity for OA-AL2b1 and OA-AL2b2 Neurons in Drosophila melanogaster. Neuroinformatics, 24(2), 25. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12021-026-09783-4

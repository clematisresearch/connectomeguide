# Research and Your Next Steps

Research experience can also help prepare you for the **next stage of your education**.

For high school students, research can help you explore your interests, develop new skills, and prepare for university. For undergraduate students, research can help you decide whether further study is right for you and prepare for applications to **graduate programmes, professional schools, and other opportunities**.

In the following sections, we provide some more specific advice depending on where you are in your education:

* **For High School Students:** making the most of research experiences and preparing for university.
* **For Undergraduate Students:** using research to prepare for graduate school, professional school, and other next steps.

## Scientific Writing Guide

Writing a scientific report may feel unfamiliar if this is your first research project. Fortunately, many of the skills used by professional researchers can be learned through practice and guidance.

Below is a concise guide to writing a research paper produced by the **Taylor & Francis Group**, one of the world's largest academic publishing companies. The guide provides practical advice on structuring a scientific manuscript, including how to write an introduction, methods, results, discussion, abstract, and title, as well as tips for communicating research clearly and effectively.

**Source:** https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Writing_your_paper_ebook.pdf

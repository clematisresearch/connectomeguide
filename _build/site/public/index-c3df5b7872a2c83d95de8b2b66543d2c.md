# Handbook

Welcome to the Conn2026 Handbook.

This section contains all the information you need to participate in the **2026 Connectomics Research Competition**, including competition rules, eligibility requirements, submission guidelines, awards, important dates, and other essential resources.

We strongly recommend reading all sections of this handbook carefully before beginning your project. Doing so will help ensure that you understand the competition requirements, maximize your learning experience, and avoid missing important deadlines or submission criteria.

If you have not yet read the main introduction page, **2026 Connectomics Research Competition**, we recommend starting there first. It provides an overview of the scientific motivation behind the competition, the datasets you will explore, and the learning resources available to support your journey.

---

## What You'll Find in This Handbook

### Competition Overview

An introduction to the competition, including an overview of the two research tracks—**Synapse-Level Connectomics (Flies)** and **Macroscale Connectomics (Humans)**. This section explains the goals of the competition, what resources are provided, and what you can expect to learn throughout the program.

### Eligibility Criteria & How to Participate

Information about who is eligible to participate, how to register, and what is required to receive a participation certificate and/or be considered for prizes and awards.

### Submitting Your Entry & Selection Criteria

An overview of the final deliverables required for the competition, including submission requirements and evaluation criteria. This section explains how projects will be assessed and what judges will look for when selecting award recipients.

### Important Dates

A summary of key deadlines and milestones throughout the competition, including registration deadlines, submission deadlines, and award announcements.

### Awards

Details about the various awards available to participants, including both monetary prizes and non-monetary forms of recognition.

### Generative AI

Guidelines and policies regarding the use of generative artificial intelligence tools throughout the competition. This section explains what forms of AI-assisted work are permitted and how participants should appropriately acknowledge their use.

### Important Links

A collection of useful links, datasets, resources, registration forms, communication channels, and other materials relevant to the competition.

### Organizers and Partners

Information about the researchers, faculty members, institutions, organizations, and volunteers who have made Conn2026 possible.

We are excited to have you join us in exploring one of the greatest scientific frontiers of our time. Whether this is your first research experience or one of many, we hope Conn2026 provides an opportunity to learn, discover, and contribute to our understanding of the brain.

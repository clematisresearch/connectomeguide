# Awards and Prizes

A total of **CAD $750** in prize money will be awarded across multiple categories in recognition of outstanding scientific inquiry, communication, and community engagement.

## Research Excellence Awards

These awards recognize submissions that demonstrate exceptional scientific reasoning, engagement with the datasets and tools, quality of analysis, and effectiveness of communication.

### High School Category

| Award       |    Prize |
| ----------- | -------: |
| First Place | CAD $200 |
| Runner-Up   | CAD $100 |

### Undergraduate Category

| Award       |    Prize |
| ----------- | -------: |
| First Place | CAD $200 |
| Runner-Up   | CAD $100 |

## Community Engagement Award

| Award                      |   Prize |
| -------------------------- | ------: |
| Community Engagement Award | CAD $50 |

This award recognizes a participant who has made an outstanding contribution to the Connectome2026 community through consistent participation in workshops, discussions, journal clubs, office hours, Discord activities, and other competition-related events.

The award celebrates participants who help foster a supportive, collaborative, and intellectually curious learning environment for others.

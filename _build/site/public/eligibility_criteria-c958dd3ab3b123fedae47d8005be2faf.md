# Eligibility Criteria

The 2026 Connectomics Research Competition is open to students worldwide and features two prize categories.

## High School Category

This category is intended for students who, on **July 1, 2026**, are:

* Currently enrolled in high school; or
* Taking a gap year after completing high school; and
* Have **not yet begun** post-secondary studies (e.g., college, university, or equivalent).

## Undergraduate Category

This category is intended for students who, on **July 1, 2026**, are:

* Currently enrolled in a post-secondary institution (e.g., college or university); and
* Have **not yet completed their first undergraduate degree**.

Students enrolled in undergraduate-entry professional programs (e.g., MBBS or equivalent programs entered directly from high school) are eligible to participate in the Undergraduate Category.

However, participants who have already completed an undergraduate degree before July 1, 2026—including those enrolled in graduate-entry medicine, graduate-entry law, master's programs, PhD programs, or other postgraduate programs—are not eligible for undergraduate prizes.

Everyone, regardless of their age and educational level, is welcome to use our resource and submit for participation certificates!

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I participate as part of a team?

No. The competition is designed as an individual learning and research experience.

One of our primary goals is to help participants develop their own scientific thinking, analytical skills, and research independence. Working individually ensures that every participant has the opportunity to engage directly with the data, develop their own ideas, and take ownership of their learning journey.

That said, we strongly encourage participants to discuss ideas, ask questions, and support one another through our community channels. Participants are welcome to engage with fellow competitors and researchers through the Clematis Discord community:

**Discord:** https://tinyurl.com/discordclematis

---

## I will begin university in August or September 2026. Which category should I compete in?

Your category is determined by your educational status on **July 1, 2026**.

If you have **not yet started** your undergraduate or post-secondary studies on or before July 1, 2026, you should participate in the **High School Category**, even if you will begin university later in 2026.

---

## I completed my high school studies before July 1, 2026 or am taking a gap year. Can I still participate in the High School Category?

Yes.

As long as you have **not started** your post-secondary education before July 1, 2026, you remain eligible for the High School Category.

This includes students who have completed their high school examinations, graduated from high school, or are currently taking a gap year before beginning university.

---

## I completed my undergraduate degree requirements in May 2026 but will officially graduate or convocate later in the year. Am I eligible for the Undergraduate Category?

You are welcome to participate in the competition, but you will not be eligible for undergraduate awards or prizes.

If you completed your final undergraduate course requirements and effectively finished your first undergraduate degree **before July 1, 2026**, you are considered to have completed your undergraduate studies for the purpose of prize eligibility, regardless of your official graduation or convocation date.

However, if you are on a summer break and have **not yet completed all degree requirements**—for example, if you still need to complete future coursework in order to graduate—you remain eligible for the Undergraduate Category.

---

## I am in my first year of CEGEP, which is often considered equivalent to Grade 12 in the American system. Am I eligible for the High School Category?

We recognize that the Quebec education system is unique, and we aim to design our eligibility criteria in a way that is both flexible and fair across different educational systems.

### Pre-University (Academic) CEGEP

If you are enrolled in a **two-year pre-university CEGEP program** leading to the Quebec *Diplôme d'études collégiales* (DEC), you will be considered part of the **High School Category** throughout the entirety of your two-year program.

This reflects the fact that many universities worldwide view pre-university CEGEP as a transitional stage between secondary and undergraduate education, and completion of the program is generally required before beginning undergraduate studies.

### Technical CEGEP

If you are enrolled in a **three-year technical CEGEP program**, your eligibility differs slightly.

You may participate in the **High School Category during your first year** of the technical CEGEP program. However, once you have begun your second year, you will no longer be eligible for the High School Category.

To be eligible for High School Category prizes, you must **not have started your second year of a technical CEGEP program before July 1, 2026**.

If you are unsure which category applies to your situation, please contact us via Discord or email at clematis.reh@gmail.com

# Important Dates

The following dates are important milestones for Connectome2026:

| Event | Date |
|---------|---------|
| **Early Feedback Opportunity** | September 30, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT-4) |
| **Final Submission Deadline** | December 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EST (GMT-5) |
| **Winners and Awardees Announced** | February 2027 |
| **Certificates and Prize Distribution** | March–May 2027 |

## Early Feedback Opportunity

Participants who submit their work by **September 30, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (GMT-4)** will receive general feedback from the organizers. This is an optional opportunity intended to help participants refine their projects before the final submission deadline. We aim to provide feedback by mid-November 2026. 

## Final Submission Deadline

All competition submissions must be received by **December 31, 2026, 5:00 PM EST (GMT-5)**.

## Winners and Awardees Announced

Competition winners and award recipients will be announced in **February 2027**.

## Certificates and Prize Distribution

Participation certificates, award certificates, and prizes will be distributed between **March and May 2027**.
